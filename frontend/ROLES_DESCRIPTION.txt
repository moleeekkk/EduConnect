                      EDUCONNECT - ROLES & PERMISSIONS DETAILS
================================================================================
Project: EduConnect (Educational Institution Management System)
Frontend Portal Role Details: Student, Teacher, and Admin
================================================================================

OVERVIEW:
EduConnect is a comprehensive web portal designed to connect Students, Teachers, 
and Administrators within an academic institution. Each user role is provided 
with a dedicated, secure dashboard and specialized feature set tailored to their 
daily academic and administrative requirements.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STUDENT ROLE (STUDENT PORTAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Route Base: /student
Primary Color Theme: Blue (#2563EB)

DESCRIPTION:
The Student Portal provides students with access to their personalized academic 
records, learning resources, attendance logs, schedules, and communication channels 
with faculty.

KEY MODULES & FEATURES:
1. Dashboard (/student/dashboard)
   - Overview of recent campus announcements and notices.
   - Quick attendance summary and academic status.
   - Upcoming class timetable summary & pending assignments alerts.

2. My Profile (/student/profile)
   - View personal information, contact details, parent info, roll number, 
     department, and enrollment status.
   - Edit/update contact information and upload profile picture.

3. Attendance (/student/attendance)
   - Subject-wise and overall attendance percentage breakdown.
   - Monthly attendance record with present/absent dates.
   - Visual progress bars and low-attendance warnings (e.g., < 75%).

4. MarkSheet & Results (/student/marksheet, /student/results)
   - Semester-wise exam performance cards and grade breakdown.
   - Marks distribution in internal exams, mid-terms, and final exams.
   - Cumulative GPA (CGPA) and percentage calculation summaries.

5. Timetable (/student/timetable)
   - Interactive weekly lecture schedule.
   - Subject details, lecture timing, classroom location, and assigned faculty name.

6. Assignments (/student/assignments)
   - View pending and completed homework/assignments uploaded by teachers.
   - Download assignment resource files and guidelines.
   - Online submission module: upload response documents/code before the deadline.
   - Track submission status, grades, and teacher feedback.

7. Weekly Tests (/student/weekly-tests)
   - Weekly test schedules and syllabus coverage info.
   - Review scores, performance analytics, and topic mastery.

8. Quiz Practice (/student/quiz-practice)
   - Subject-specific interactive multiple-choice questions (MCQs).
   - Instant scoring, answer keys, and detailed explanation review.

9. Study Material (/student/study-material)
   - Access course lecture notes, slides, reference PDFs, and recommended links.
   - Organized by subject, topic, and semester.

10. Old Question Papers (/student/old-papers)
    - Library of previous years' university and midterm exam papers.
    - Downloadable PDFs filtered by semester, year, and subject.

11. Digital ID (/student/digital-id)
    - Virtual digital student identity card.
    - Displays Student Photo, Name, Roll Number, Department, Valid Period, and QR code.

12. Leave Request (/student/leave-request)
    - Apply for student leave (Medical, Personal, etc.).
    - Specify start/end dates and leave reasons.
    - Track approval status in real-time (Pending, Approved, Rejected by Teacher).

13. Ask Doubts (/student/doubts)
    - Submit academic queries and doubts directly to subject teachers.
    - Attach images/files related to the doubt.
    - View teacher replies and discussion threads.

2. TEACHER ROLE (TEACHER PORTAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Route Base: /teacher
Primary Color Theme: Teal (#14B8A6)

DESCRIPTION:
The Teacher Portal enables faculty members to efficiently manage classrooms, track 
student attendance, grade assignments, publish study content, handle student leave 
applications, and communicate directly with students and parents.

KEY MODULES & FEATURES:
1. Dashboard (/teacher/dashboard)
   - Daily class schedule overview and quick actions.
   - Pending leave applications requiring review.
   - Unresolved student doubts alerts.
   - Important faculty notices and announcements.

2. My Profile (/teacher/profile)
   - View faculty credentials, employee ID, assigned subjects, and qualifications.
   - Update contact details and profile avatar.

3. Manage Students (/teacher/students)
   - Complete directory of students in assigned classes/departments.
   - Search, filter, and view individual student academic & attendance history.

4. Mark Attendance (/teacher/attendance)
   - Daily/lecture-wise student attendance marking interface.
   - Mark status: Present, Absent, or Late for individual students.
   - Batch save attendance records and edit historical entries.

5. Upload Assignment & Grading (/teacher/assignments, /teacher/grades)
   - Create and publish new assignments with due dates and attachments.
   - Review student submissions online.
   - Grade submissions and provide comments/feedback.

6. Weekly Tests (/teacher/weekly-tests)
   - Schedule weekly tests for assigned classes.
   - Upload syllabus guidelines and record student test scores.

7. Quiz Practice (/teacher/quiz-practice)
   - Build MCQ quizzes for practice.
   - Manage question banks, options, and correct answers per subject.

8. Study Material (/teacher/study-material)
   - Upload lecture notes, PDF documents, and study resources for students.
   - Categorize by subject, unit, and batch.

9. Old Question Papers (/teacher/old-papers)
   - Upload past examination papers for student preparation.

10. Reply Doubts (/teacher/reply-doubts)
    - View student-submitted academic questions.
    - Provide text explanations and attach supporting solution files.
    - Mark doubts as resolved.

11. Leave Management (/teacher/leave-management)
    - Review leave applications submitted by assigned students.
    - Check leave reasons and date range.
    - Action buttons: Approve or Reject with teacher remarks.

12. Parent Email (/teacher/parent-email)
    - Direct communication channel with parents/guardians.
    - Send automated or manual emails regarding attendance warnings, academic progress, or misconduct alerts.

3. ADMIN ROLE (ADMIN PORTAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Route Base: /admin
Primary Color Theme: Amber/Gold (#F59E0B)

DESCRIPTION:
The Admin Portal provides institutional administrators with total control over system 
configuration, user management (students & teachers), academic programs, course 
structures, subjects, and institutional analytics.

KEY MODULES & FEATURES:
1. Dashboard (/admin/dashboard)
   - High-level institutional metrics: Total Students, Total Faculty, Active Courses, 
     Departments, and Campus count.
   - System health indicators and platform usage statistics.

2. User Management (/admin/users, /admin/students, /admin/teachers)
   - Complete CRUD operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete) for Student & Teacher accounts.
   - Add new students/teachers individually or bulk import via CSV/Excel.
   - Activate/Deactivate user access, reset passwords, and assign roles.

3. Institute Management (/admin/institutes)
   - Manage institutional details, campuses, affiliated colleges, and branch locations.

4. Department & Program Management (/admin/programs, /admin/courses)
   - Create & manage academic departments (e.g., Computer Science, IT).
   - Configure programs/degrees (e.g., MCA, BCA, B.Tech), program duration, and semester structures.

5. Subject Management (/admin/subjects)
   - Create and define curriculum subjects.
   - Set credit hours, subject codes, and semester mapping.
   - Assign primary and secondary teachers to specific subjects.

6. Reports & Analytics (/admin/reports)
   - Comprehensive institutional analytical reports:
     * Campus-wide attendance trends.
     * Department-wise academic result metrics.
     * Faculty performance & syllabus progress.
   - Export reports in PDF and Excel formats.

7. My Profile (/admin/profile)
   - Administrator profile settings, security credentials, and account details.
